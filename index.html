What is a Jayagoo spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Jayagoo spreadsheet means a shopping shortlist organized into rows for product links, categories, sizes, prices and comparison notes. This page explains that method; it does not provide an official Jayagoo spreadsheet or a downloadable product database.

02 / A repeatable method

Your Jayagoo spreadsheet guide:
save, compare, decide.

A manageable list is easier to revisit than a collection of disconnected tabs.

01.

Choose one category.

Start with a specific need: everyday sneakers, a warmer layer or a simple accessory. Save the destination listing link and use a descriptive item name.

02.

Make details comparable.

Record the same fields for similar items. Keep the selected size, color, currency and checking date beside the price so your notes retain their context.

03.

Check before committing.

Revisit the listing to confirm the chosen variation, measurements, stock and final costs. Mark unanswered questions rather than treating missing information as confirmed.

03 / Compare with context

Different categories.
Different questions.

Use the details relevant to the product rather than applying one generic checklist to everything.

Jayagoo spreadsheet shoes

A shoe shortlist should identify the exact variation and the information available about fit.

  • Record the listed sizing system: EU, UK, US or centimeters.
  • Keep the selected colorway and model description together.
  • Check upper, lining and sole descriptions in the actual listing.
  • Ask for clarification when sizing information is incomplete.

Jayagoo spreadsheet clothing

Compare garment measurements with an item you already own and like wearing.

  • For tops, record chest, shoulders, length and sleeves where provided.
  • For pants and shorts, compare waist, rise, inseam and leg opening.
  • For sets, check top and bottom measurements separately.
  • Distinguish a stated fabric composition from a visual impression.

Jayagoo spreadsheet accessories

Small pieces need precise notes, especially where size and skin contact matter.

  • Check bracelet circumference, ring sizing and necklace length.
  • Record the stated base material and surface finish separately.
  • For headwear, look for circumference and adjustment details.
  • Review the actual seller’s care information and return terms.

Keep the total in view

A displayed item price is only one part of a purchasing decision.

  • Use one clearly labeled currency per price column.
  • Keep delivery estimates separate from confirmed charges.
  • Leave unknown costs blank or mark them “to confirm.”
  • Recheck your final selection and payable total before ordering.

A few practical answers about this guide, sizing, prices and the visual references.

Is this an official Jayagoo website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS, and it does not claim affiliation with Jayagoo or the brands pictured.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item you own. Record chest, shoulder, length, waist and inseam as relevant, keeping centimeters and inches in separate labeled columns.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping?

Do not assume it does. Record the item price, currency, delivery charges and any other applicable costs separately, and confirm the final total with the seller or service before ordering.

Are the pictured products available on CAFANS?

The photographs are editorial visual references. They do not confirm CAFANS stock, prices, authenticity or availability. Check the actual destination listing before making a purchasing decision.

Where can I browse the categories mentioned here?
